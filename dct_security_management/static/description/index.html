DCT Security Operations

Connected security company workflows for Odoo 19 Community

Manage native employees as guards, customer sites, posts and effective service contracts. Plan coverage, publish individual assignments and reconcile actual attendance.

  • Weekly planning board, calendar, attendance review and replacement history.
  • Incident investigations, manual patrol records and supervisor inspections.
  • Operational dashboard and a configurable site map with a list fallback.
  • Reviewed payroll worksheets and draft native customer invoices.
  • Role, company and supervisor site restrictions; English and Arabic support.
  • Eight printable reports and optional draft generation and review reminders.

Configure roles, company policies and financial accounts before use. Map tiles require a provider URL and attribution. Payroll worksheets require a separate localization for statutory taxes and contributions. See the included README for setup and validation.
